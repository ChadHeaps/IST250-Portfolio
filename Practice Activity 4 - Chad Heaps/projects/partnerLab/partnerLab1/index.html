Penn State's IST Certificate

Welcome to the information center for the Information Science and Technology Certificate. Below, you will be able to learn more about the courses that you will need to earn this certificate while attending Penn State.

What is this Certificate?

Penn State's College of IST offers a program that allows students to introduce themselves to the world of IT. While a minor is available, this option allows students to learn the fundamentals of the subject and apply what they have learned when leaving college.

Please Note: As of Fall 2026, Penn State will no longer offer this certificate to new students. If interested, please explore the new majors, minors, and certificates on their official website.

Why should you take this Certificate?

In our currently rapidly evolving world of technology, any career path should have a foundation in technology to help stand out in an application. This certificate provides the fundamentals that a person should have to be able to understand and work with the technology around them. This certificate is also highly recommended for anyone in any major with a love for technology!

What is required to earn this certificate?

The IST Certificate requires 12 credits.

  • IST 110
  • IST 210
  • IST 220
  • IST 250
